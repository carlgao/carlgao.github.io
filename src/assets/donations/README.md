Downloaded from official sources on 2026-11-10. These are unchanged originals; preserve their aspect ratios and clear space when displaying them.

Use Venmo's wordmark, rather than its V monogram, which its current guidelines reserve for social identity. The white wordmark is supplied for dark backgrounds; Venmo's brand blue is `#008CFF`.

Display PayPal's color mark on a light background. Display Zelle's white wordmark on purple; its official website uses primary purple `#6d1ed4` and header purple `#471e99`. Keep readable platform labels and accessible names with the controls.
